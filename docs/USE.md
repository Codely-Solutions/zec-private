# Using ZEC Private

ZEC Private is an independent open-source wallet, not operated or supported by Zodl. It is provided under the [MIT license](../LICENSE), without warranty or a recovery service.

Keep your recovery phrase and wallet birthday secure before resetting or replacing a wallet. Never enter a hardware-wallet recovery phrase into this app.

Transactions may be irreversible. Verify the asset, network, destination, amount and fees before approval. Swap providers have their own terms and refund procedures; keep swap records until settlement or refund completes.

See the [wallet guide](WALLET-GUIDE.md) for instructions and [privacy](PRIVACY.md) before sharing diagnostics.
