# Using ZEC Private

This is an independent open-source wallet fork. It is not operated or supported by Zodl. See LICENSE and the bundled dependency notices for applicable licenses and attribution. No separate upstream product terms are represented as terms for this fork.

You control wallet recovery and transaction approval. Preserve the recovery phrase and birthday securely before resetting or replacing a wallet. Hardware and view-only accounts have their own recovery and signing requirements. Never enter a hardware-wallet seed into this app.

Transactions may be irreversible. Verify the asset, network, destination, amount and fees before approval. Third-party swap services have their own conditions and failure/refund procedures; settlement is not guaranteed. No warranty or recovery service is promised. Keep accepted swap records until settlement or refund completes.

The app can export diagnostics using Android sharing. Choose the recipient yourself and inspect sensitive content first. No staffed support inbox is configured. Do not post secret-bearing exports to GitHub. GitHub release and documentation links require repository access.
