# Swap amounts and costs

The quote is an estimate of output. Its minimum output is the provider's base-unit guarantee converted using the destination asset's decimals. Token amounts, deposit totals and that minimum retain exact asset precision; compact fiat formatting is not suitable for a token guarantee because it may round upward. The review shows the minimum for floating-output swaps.

For incoming FLEX_INPUT, expandable **Quote details** shows the provider's positive `minAmountIn` at exact origin-asset precision. The minimum must reach the deposit address before the quote expires. The deposit screen displays and copies the suggested `amountIn`.

At funding admission, estimated amounts and minima must be positive integers in the corresponding base units, and each minimum must be at most its estimate. The quote must use SIMPLE deposit instructions with the requested origin-chain deposit and refund and destination-chain recipient. A deposit memo or additional recipient instruction blocks funding. [1Click quote contract](https://docs.near-intents.org/api-reference/oneclick/request-a-swap-quote)

The 1Click boundary limits decimal text and the absolute wire exponent to 512, precision and expanded integer width to 128 digits, and scale to −127 through 320. Token metadata accepts 0 through 255 decimals. These limits leave room for the 78 digits of an unsigned 256-bit atomic amount, every ERC-20 `uint8` decimal count and fractional USD estimates. Decimal values keep their exact precision. The budget covers quote and status decimals, catalog prices and minimum-amount error hints. An over-budget value fails the existing provider request or refresh path. Saved swap metadata remains readable through its existing serializer. [Token amount representation](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-20), [1Click token metadata](https://docs.near-intents.org/api-reference/oneclick/get-supported-tokens), [1Click status amounts](https://docs.near-intents.org/api-reference/oneclick/check-swap-execution-status)

For EXACT_OUTPUT, `amountIn` is the deposit including the input allowance; `minAmountIn` is the minimum input, not a maximum. Both must be positive integer atomic amounts, with `minAmountIn <= amountIn` and `minAmountIn >= floor(amountIn × (10000 − requestedBps) / 10000)`. The included allowance is their difference. Its ZEC and fiat values share the quote's input valuation; the explanatory row never increases the funded total. Fund the exact amount reviewed. See the provider's [quote contract](https://docs.near-intents.org/api-reference/oneclick/get-quote).

CrossPay's exact output must be representable at the selected token's precision. Positive subunit or over-precision input shows **Invalid amount** and disables Review. The request use case and DTO independently require a positive whole base-unit amount, and serialization emits its integer string. Exact-input and flexible-input conversions retain their existing deliberate rounding. `CrossPayAtomicAmountTest` follows the production parser, form, request, data source and serializer with pinned assets at 6, 8, 18 and 24 decimals. `NearSwapDataSourceTest` covers the wire conversion at zero decimals.

A payment URI prefills the token and fiat fields for the compatible selected asset. An untouched prefill belongs to that URI and asset. Changing the payment or asset clears the prior prefill. A compatible replacement URI can supply a new amount, and returning to the original asset can restore the unchanged URI's amount. Editing either amount field makes the amount user-entered.

Status output comes from the provider's settled amount when available, including raw base-unit output when the formatted field is absent. Encrypted metadata keeps the reviewed quote and provider execution separately. Sparse refreshes retain known execution across restart; equal timestamps may fill missing execution but cannot replace a conflicting known value. Legacy saved amounts retain unknown provenance until an actual execution field arrives. Activity and both Detail paths distinguish quoted, saved unknown and provider-reported values. A missing USD execution valuation is not replaced by a quote valuation. Otherwise the displayed value remains explicitly quoted. Provider output is not an independently measured wallet credit. The wallet's received transaction records its own scanned net value. A provider/bridge reporting difference must not be silently corrected with a guessed constant.

Resolve a status response by its complete exact provider asset ID first. Use a documented alias only when the exact ID is absent and the alias resolves uniquely. Coexisting exact and aliased catalog entries remain distinct selections; genuinely duplicated exact IDs and ambiguous fallback matches remain errors. Saved swap metadata provides display-only recovery while a fresh status is unavailable. Mark it as saved and retain request/response identity checks for new data.

The fork submits no app fees. Current unauthenticated 1Click requests use EXACT_INPUT, EXACT_OUTPUT or FLEX_INPUT. NEAR documents a 0.25% platform charge on non-ANY_INPUT quotes, included in the returned quote. This is not the total swap cost. Protocol, route and bridge costs or favourable execution may also affect output. Do not subtract an included fee a second time or infer aggregate fees from independently priced fiat values.

The review omits a wallet fee for externally funded incoming swaps. Outgoing swaps retain the wallet proposal fee; completed outgoing details use the actual wallet transaction fee rather than historical aggregate metadata. Legacy stored fee fields remain readable for compatibility.

Provider success becomes review-ready only after local funding preparation succeeds. The request and cancel-sheet dismissal share one navigation claim. Outgoing review requires the matching prepared proposal, and submission rechecks that exact proposal after authentication. A changed or missing proposal requires a fresh review. Incoming externally funded swaps retain their separate confirmation path.

Software and hardware submission share a durable funding reservation after final proposal/signature validation and before the SDK call. Canceling hardware signing before submission leaves no reservation and permits fresh approval of the same prepared review. Once reserved, an uncertain submission keeps its tracking record and blocks another funding attempt; inspect that swap's status before recovery. Revalidate the reviewed proposal across key access, tracking writes, PCZT redaction and proof generation, and pass the exact signed Keystone proposal to submission.

Keep complete provider identities for status recovery independently of live prices and the curated offer list. New selections must match the pinned ID, symbol, network, decimals and contract in `SwapPriceMarkets`; historical exact identities and the existing unique alias fallback remain available for recovery. Validate the echoed deposit reference before mapping or persisting a status. Activity details show a non-actionable **Deposit reference**; current funding controls belong to the expiring deposit screen.

New tracking records bind the exact echoed asset IDs, swap mode, recipient, refund and deposit reference to the reviewed quote. Records created before this binding use the existing deposit and asset-pair checks. Refreshes for one deposit run in order, and storage rejects older provider updates and changes to completed settlement or refund outcomes. The swap creation time continues to order Activity. [1Click status contract](https://docs.near-intents.org/api-reference/oneclick/check-swap-execution-status)

Accepted routes also retain the original provider response when its non-dry signature verifies against the documented manager key. The verifier follows the provider SDK's field selection, stable ordering, SHA-256/Base58 message and Ed25519 signature; the signature does not cover every accompanying response field. Evidence is limited to 16 KiB per response and the newest 64 evidence-bearing records per account, inside existing encrypted wallet metadata. Repeated tracking and status refresh preserve the first original. Legacy, oversized or unverifiable responses retain ordinary route validation and tracking without signed evidence. Key rotation, unsupported numeric canonicalization and provider dispute-intake requirements remain external limits. [Provider retention contract](https://docs.near-intents.org/api-reference/oneclick/request-a-swap-quote#response), [signature contract](https://docs.near-intents.org/integration/distribution-channels/1click-api/verify-quote-signature), [official verifier](https://github.com/defuse-protocol/one-click-sdk-typescript/blob/main/src/quote-signature.ts)

The support sheet's separate **Share original quote** action explains that it contains private addresses and amounts. It captures the current app-access grant, rereads durable metadata and rechecks that grant, account, wallet generation and original route immediately before opening Android's chooser. Relocking revokes pending reads and old callbacks even after a later unlock. Saved evidence remains reachable during a provider outage. Ordinary issue reports and public diagnostics exclude the original body. Reset follows the user's existing encrypted-metadata retention choice.

After a refused refresh, the wallet can reload the matching entry for the original account and wallet session. It publishes the canonical snapshot only after syncing its directory, since a failed sync can leave a replacement file readable. An unchanged saved status and amount leave the accepted live details visible.

The signed **Value difference** compares received USD with total USD to fund, including known separately paid wallet fees. Quoted costs are already reflected once. It uses the coherent quote snapshot, includes an EXACT_OUTPUT allowance and assumes no future refund. It excludes unknown external-wallet fees, hides on missing/non-positive values or quote expiry, and is not independent market-price verification.

Show only positive provider-reported refunds, including successful swaps. Never derive a refund from deposit minus execution. The provider reports origin/destination transaction lists without a dedicated refund-hash field; retain neutral chain labels for ambiguous origin transactions, preserve the wallet-known deposit separately, and keep copy-ID for unsupported explorer chains.

Official rules: https://docs.near-intents.org/resources/fees (reviewed 2026-11-07). Provider status is evidence of its report, not proof of shielded output value. Personal examples and raw provider responses stay outside this public repository.

Upstream integrations must preserve amount provenance, base-unit conversion, zero app fees, actual wallet fee presentation and history amount search. Deterministic regressions and real Tor quote acceptance cover these boundaries. Funded production settlement is outside automated acceptance.
